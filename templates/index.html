Questions & Answers

Is Mentics completely free?

Yes, our early access tier is completely free. You get access to unlimited AI path generation, essay feedback, and the full dashboard without a credit card.

How does the AI mentor work?

Our AI analyzes your performance data and learning style to generate specific study paths. You can also chat with it directly for tutoring on specific subjects, test prep, or college essay reviews.

Can I track both SAT and ACT?

Absolutely. Mentics supports adaptive learning paths and analytics for both the SAT and ACT, allowing you to focus on the test that best suits your strengths.
