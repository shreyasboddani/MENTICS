BUILT AROUND REAL PROGRESS

ACT planning that changes when your situation does

A study schedule made once can become irrelevant after a new practice test, a missed week, or a changed test date. Mentics keeps paths short on purpose. Five steps are enough to create direction, and the next cycle can respond to the results instead of dragging an outdated plan forward.
