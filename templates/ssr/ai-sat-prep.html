QUESTIONS

Know before you start.

Does Mentics generate a new plan as I improve?

Yes. New paths can use completed work, measured skill mastery, logged scores, and mistake patterns so the focus changes with you.

Does the AI replace official SAT material?

No. Mentics provides original SAT-style learning and practice. Students should also use official materials and guidance from College Board.

Can I use Mentics before I have a score?

Yes. Your first unit can begin from your goals and self-reported weaknesses, then become more precise as answer data accumulates.
